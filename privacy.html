Skyleaf privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Skyleaf is a learning app for children ages 3 to 8, created by Samba Siva Rao. It works without an account and without an internet connection.

What Skyleaf does not do

  • No advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, or tracking.
  • No collection of names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, or voice recordings.
  • No sending of child profiles, progress, settings, or artwork to any server.
  • No links to other websites, apps, or social media inside the app.
  • No camera, microphone, location, contacts, or advertising permissions.

What stays on the device

Child profiles (a first name or nickname, an age, and an avatar), learning progress, favourites, settings, and saved artwork are stored only on the device. Images a parent chooses to import stay in the app's own storage. Speech and sounds are played on the device.

Managing and deleting data

A parent can delete a child profile and its learning history from the parent area. The app asks for confirmation before progress is reset or a profile is deleted. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are turned off so child data is not copied off the device.

The website version

The browser version at this address runs the same app. Its data is kept in the browser's own storage on that device and is not sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data in the browser removes it. GitHub, which hosts these pages, may keep standard web server logs; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Changes

If accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or other online services are ever added, this policy and the in-app notice will be updated first, and no child data will be sent until suitable parental consent is in place.
